## Prompt final

Una llamada por cada ilustración, usando como objetivo su respectivo archivo original `metodo-*.webp` y `transparent_background: true`.

> Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: the supplied square illustration. Remove only the lavender/purple square background and its ground/drop shadows, producing a clean cutout on a genuinely transparent alpha background. Preserve all illustrated foreground objects, their original placement, shapes, dark outlines, colors and friendly flat cartoon style. Keep purple details belonging to the foreground objects. Do not add objects, text, framing, borders or any new background. Keep the complete illustration uncropped, centered in a square canvas, suitable for a small website process-step illustration.
